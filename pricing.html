Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is there a setup fee?

No. There's no setup fee on either plan — you start your 3-month free trial and only add payment once you're ready to continue.

Can I switch between Solo and Clinic later?

Yes. If a solo caseload grows past what Solo supports, or a clinic wants to simplify down to one BCBA, just reach out and we'll move your account to the right plan.

Is there a contract or cancellation fee?

No. Both plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime with no penalty.

What happens after the 3-month free trial?

We'll let you know before it ends. If you'd like to continue, you add a payment method and billing starts at your plan's normal rate — nothing is charged automatically without you setting that up.

Do you sign Business Associate Agreements (BAAs)?

Yes — we'll sign a BAA as part of onboarding, before any client data goes into the system.
